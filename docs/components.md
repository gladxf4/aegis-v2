## Circuit templates

`circuits/age_proof.circom` includes:

- `poseidon.circom` — Poseidon hash;
- `comparators.circom` — `LessEqThan`, `GreaterEqThan`;
- `eddsaposeidon.circom` — EdDSA verification and its dependencies
  (`escalarmulany`, `escalarmulfix`, `bitify`, `compconstant`, `babyjub`).

These files come from Circomlib and carry GPL-3.0 terms. Keep the upstream notices
when redistributing the circuit or compiled artifacts.
