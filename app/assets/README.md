# AegisID artwork

The app's visual assets:

- `aegis-logo.png` is the brand image supplied for the project.
- `wallet-card.png` shows a sample credential staying with its holder's device.
- `proof-route.png` shows a credential moving from a device to a verifier as a proof.
- `verification-states.png` is the supplied check/pause illustration; `verified-mark.png`
  and `review-mark.png` are the cropped status marks used in results.
- `../favicon.svg` is a compact shield-and-checkmark mark for the browser tab and header.

All raster images are local. The page loads no external images or fonts.
